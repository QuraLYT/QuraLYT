QuraLYT is currently in active development. ## 🕌 About

QuraLYT is a Qur'an platform created to help Muslims build a deeper connection with the words of Allah through a calm, respectful, distraction-free experience. Every screen, interaction, and feature is carefully designed to reduce digital noise and make reading, listening, learning, and reflecting feel natural and peaceful.

QuraLYT exists to build one of the world's most respectful and accessible Qur'an platforms — one where the Qur'an remains the center of the experience, not the app itself.

> QuraLYT is not just a Qur'an app. It is a long-term platform dedicated to helping Muslims read, understand, listen to, and reflect on the Qur'an through thoughtful design, accessibility, and authentic Islamic resources.

> To build one of the world's most trusted, accessible, and respectful digital Qur'an platforms for every Muslim.

QuraLYT combines **"Qur'an"** with **"LYT"** from LYTDesign™, reflecting our mission to bring clarity, thoughtful design, and accessibility to the experience of reading and reflecting on the Noble Qur'an.

## 🎯 Why QuraLYT Exists

Many Qur'an apps are cluttered with ads, subscriptions, and data collection. QuraLYT was built with one clear purpose: to let the Qur'an remain the center of the experience — free, private, and accessible to everyone, regardless of age, ability, language, or income.

> "Technology should never compete for attention. It should quietly support worship."

## 🌟 What Makes QuraLYT Different?

QuraLYT is more than a Qur'an reader. It is a platform designed from the ground up around respect for the Noble Qur'an and the needs of Muslims of every age and ability. Unlike many applications, accessibility is not an afterthought — it is part of the foundation of every screen, interaction, and feature.

**Built Around Accessibility**
Designed from the beginning for elderly users, people with disabilities, and neurodivergent readers — with high contrast themes, scalable text, simplified navigation, and thoughtful interaction patterns.

**A Complete Theming System**
Carefully designed themes that improve comfort while reading. Every theme is created to remain elegant, readable, and distraction-free.

**Beautiful Surah Artwork**
Every Surah is paired with original artwork created to encourage reflection while maintaining respect for the Qur'an. Artwork complements the reading experience rather than distracting from it — one of QuraLYT's defining features, and something few Qur'an apps offer.

**Thoughtful Interaction Design**
- **LYTSnap** — touch-hold to select up to 5 ayahs, then share as an image, as text, or as an AI reflection
- **LYTTouch** — touch-hold anywhere in the app to reveal contextual help exactly when it's needed

**Privacy First**
No advertising. No tracking. Local-first architecture wherever possible. Your reading remains your own.

**Built for the Future**
QuraLYT is designed as a long-term platform that will continue to grow with additional authenticated translations, audio recitations, tafsir resources, hadith, learning tools, AI-assisted reflection, accessibility improvements, and support for more devices and languages.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

QuraLYT's visual and interaction design follows a few deliberate rules:

- **A calm interface** — nothing fights for attention that shouldn't
- **Minimal distractions** — no clutter, no unnecessary motion, no dark patterns
- **Comfortable typography** — text sized and spaced for genuinely long reading sessions, not just quick glances
- **Reading-first design** — every screen is built around the Qur'an text itself, not around features
- **Respect for the Qur'an** — visual choices (color, spacing, artwork) are made to support reverence, never to entertain or distract from it

## ♿ Accessibility

Accessibility is not an extra feature — it is a core design principle, considered from the very first screen, not added afterward. QuraLYT is built with the needs of:

- **Elderly readers** — larger text options, simplified navigation, Elderly Mode
- **Users with low vision** — high-contrast themes, scalable typography
- **Users with motor impairments** — simplified interaction patterns, forgiving touch targets
- **Neurodivergent users** — calm, predictable layouts with minimal distraction

QuraLYT is built in pursuit of WCAG 2.2 AA standards.

*(Compliance badges will only be published once formally audited — we do not overclaim accessibility before it is verified.)*

---

## 📴 Offline-First

QuraLYT is designed to work fully offline wherever possible, for three reasons:

- **Privacy** — your reading activity doesn't need to touch a server to happen
- **Reliability** — the Qur'an should be readable without depending on an internet connection
- **Respect for access** — not every user has consistent connectivity, and the Qur'an shouldn't be locked behind it
